Leonidas Christos Karagkounis

PhD candidate in Computer Science, University of Macedonia

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia in Thessaloniki. I work in the MSN Lab with Prof. Ioannis Mavridis.

My thesis is about explainable AI in security operations centers. Analysts there work through a constant stream of alerts, and the machine-learning models that raise many of them rarely say why. I study explanations that adapt to the analyst using them, so that people can act on real threats sooner and can tell when the model should not be trusted.

Before the PhD I studied mathematics at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, then computer science and later data science at the Hellenic Open University. I also consult and provide research services in cybersecurity and AI.
