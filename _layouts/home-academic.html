I am a doctoral candidate in Computer Science at the Department of Applied Informatics, University of Macedonia, Thessaloniki, and a member of the MSN Lab, where I am supervised by Prof. Ioannis Mavridis.

My research concerns human-centric explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) for security operations centers (SOCs). I investigate adaptive explanation methods for machine-learning-based detection, with the aim of supporting analysts in the detection of and response to advanced persistent threats, and of fostering appropriately calibrated trust in automated decisions. Further details are given on the research page.

I hold an MSc in Data Science and Machine Learning (2025) and a BSc in Computer Science (2022) from the Hellenic Open University, and previously studied Mathematics at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. Alongside my doctoral studies, I provide independent research and consulting services in cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.
