Doctoral thesis

Human-Centric Adaptive Explainable AI in Security Operations Centers

My work examines how adaptive models and explanations can support security analysts in detecting and responding to advanced persistent threats. I am supervised by Prof. Ioannis Mavridis at the University of Macedonia's MSN Lab.

Research interests

  • Adaptive explainable AI and trust calibration in decision support
  • Reinforcement learning for cyber defense and personalized instruction
  • Intelligent tutoring systems in adversarial settings
  • Neuro-symbolic methods combining statistical models with logical constraints

Research outputs

Ongoing manuscript and code repositories are private while the work is in progress. Publications and presentations will be added when available.
