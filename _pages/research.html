Doctoral thesis

Human-Centric Adaptive Explainable AI in Security Operations Centers

Security analysts rely more and more on machine-learning models to flag suspicious activity, but those models seldom explain their output in a form an analyst can check. My thesis looks at how explanations, and the models behind them, can adapt to the person using them, and whether that helps analysts detect and respond to advanced persistent threats.

I am supervised by Prof. Ioannis Mavridis in the MSN Lab, Department of Applied Informatics, University of Macedonia. The advisory committee also includes Asst. Prof. Eftychios Protopapadakis and Asst. Prof. Nikolaos Partarakis.

Interests

  • Adaptive explanations for decision support, and how they shape an analyst's trust in a model
  • Reinforcement learning for cyber defense and for personalized instruction
  • Intelligent tutoring systems in adversarial settings
  • Neuro-symbolic methods that combine learned models with logical constraints
