01 / DOCTORAL WORK

Human-Centric Adaptive Explainable AI in Security Operations Centers

My doctoral work investigates how adaptive and explainable AI can support security analysts facing advanced persistent threats. The focus is on useful explanations, changing operational conditions, and the needs of the people making security decisions.
